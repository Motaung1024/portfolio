03 · Work

Projects

Morabaraba

morabaraba.dev

A full digital version of the traditional Southern African board game, played against the computer, a friend on the same phone, or real people online. I own all of it: product, game engine, backend, infrastructure and data.

  • Computer opponent that searches ahead in a Web Worker and keeps thinking on the player's time. It learns openings from real games, and I tune it with self-play and A/B tests.
  • Live online play with matchmaking, a move clock, and results that both players' devices must agree on before they count.
  • Accounts, leaderboards, friends and tournaments, with no sign-up wall: a name and a PIN.
  • Installable web app that works on phones as a home-screen app.
